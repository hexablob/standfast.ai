## Why this exists

Standfast is open source: the server under AGPL-3.0-only, the client packages
under Apache-2.0. That is not changing.

This agreement asks contributors to grant an additional licence to the project
owner, on top of the open source licence. It does two things:

1. It lets Standfast offer a **commercial licence** to organisations whose legal
   policy forbids the AGPL. Without it, such an organisation cannot use Standfast
   at all, however much it wants to — and neither the project nor the user
   gains anything from that outcome.
2. It gives an explicit **patent grant**, so a contributor cannot later assert
   a patent against the software they contributed to.

It does **not** take your copyright away. You keep every right you had. You are
granting a licence, not signing your work over.

It does not make Standfast less open. Every line contributed under this agreement
is still published under the open source licence above, and that cannot be
retracted for code already released.

## How to sign

Comment on your first pull request with:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.
```

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, say so, and have someone
authorised to bind the company confirm it in the same thread.

## If you would rather not sign

That is a legitimate position and it is held in good faith by people whose work
this project benefits from.

Open an issue describing the change instead. A well-written issue that leads to
an implementation is a real contribution, and it is credited as one.
